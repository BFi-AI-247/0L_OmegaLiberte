## What changed
- **[prompts/setup.md](../prompts/setup.md) now starts people reading right away** on https://digifil.de/kompass/: your AI asks about interests and hands over a pack link; without an AI, the welcome screen does the same in three steps.
- **[prompts/host.md](../prompts/host.md)** is the former guide for running your own hub (web space or own computer), unchanged in substance.
- The first question offers three ways: just start reading (recommended), run your own hub, or developer.

## Why
Most people want to read, not to operate a server. Asking everyone to install first lost them at step one. Running your own hub stays one question away, and moving later is one link (*Share my pack*).

## Adopt it
Running another public instance? Copy setup.md and replace `https://digifil.de/kompass/` with your address.
