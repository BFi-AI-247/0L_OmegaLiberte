## What changed
OmegaLiberte starts as an open recipe, extracted from the reference instance (v0.24). Built 17–20 Oct 2026 by Björn with several AI chats (Claude for building, Mistral for research and texts) coordinating through files in a repo.

## Why
> O-Ton: „Wie kriegen wir ein quasi freies Ökosystem hin, in dem nicht digifil.de/kompass führend ist, sondern das Konzept zwischen Menschen und KIs fließen kann.“

## Effect
Lessons from the first days, worth adopting anywhere:
- **Merging creators** across Mastodon, Bluesky, websites and YouTube without AI (verified links, own domains, mutual profile links) makes the source list readable.
- **Same post on several services** → one post with switchable versions (shared article URL + author relation).
- **AI selection must never split one story** into several picks; ask for `also` and check client-side.
- **AI must not invent sources** — let it name official pages, then verify every address automatically (test bench). In a first run, 23 of 44 AI-suggested sources were real.
- **Bring your own AI** (OpenRouter sign-in, Gemini key, local in-browser model) beats a shared operator key; keys encrypted on device, never synced.
- **Load older posts** per creator on demand (Mastodon `max_id`, Bluesky `cursor`); feeds only offer their fixed window — say so and link the source.
