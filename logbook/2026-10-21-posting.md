## What changed
From reading to taking part: KOMPASS can post, share with a comment and reply on **Mastodon** (OAuth with PKCE, no password) and **Bluesky** (app password). Code: `reference/web/posten/`.

## Why
> O-Ton (R008): „Posten ist der erste Schritt von ‚lesen‘ zu ‚mitreden‘. Wichtig ist, dass es leise bleibt: kein Reichweiten-Druck, kein Zählen.“

## Effect (ideally measurable)
- Never automatic: a visible preview before every send; already sent targets are never sent twice on retry. No numbers on own posts.
- Access stays on the device: Mastodon token and Bluesky refresh token in the encrypted vault, Bluesky access token only in memory, nothing in sync.
- 26 checks against staged servers (connect, PKCE, send, share, reply on both networks, expired sign-in, disconnect, UTF-8 byte offsets for Bluesky link facets with umlauts and emoji). Without a connection all existing views are pixel-identical.

## How to adopt
`posten/posten.js` + `posten/posten.css`; hooks: `postenKnoepfe(p)` in the card footer (`social/karten.js`), `postenBox()` in settings. Needs a secure context (https or localhost) — the vault uses WebCrypto. Bluesky accounts on their own PDS are not covered yet (sign-in goes through bsky.social).
